# Archive provenance

This is a curated public archive, not a claim of authorship over third-party libraries or course handouts. Author-provided coursework has been selected from a separately preserved local collection. Existing attributions in included code are retained subject to the identity and contact redactions requested for this public edition.

Office transcripts omit embedded images and may omit equations. Read an accompanying redacted PDF where available. Files withheld for privacy review remain in the private local originals, not in Git history.
