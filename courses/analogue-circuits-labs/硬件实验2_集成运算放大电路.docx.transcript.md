3.Experiment principles

The integrated operational amplifier is a high gain, high input impedance and low output impedance DC amplifier. If you add a feedback network on it, you can obtain a variety of different circuit functions. For example, applying a linear negative feedback network on it can obtain amplification functions, as well as addition, subtraction, differentiation, integration, and other analog computing functions.
