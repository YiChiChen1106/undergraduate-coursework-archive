飞控flight controller（153词）：

The PIX2.4.8 flight controller is central to this drone design project, responsible for Task 1 (Take-off), Task 2 (Tunnelling), Task 3 (Obstacle avoidance), Task 4 (Package search and pickup), Task 5 (Marker search and precise package drop-off), and Task 6 (Landing and wireless communication). Its advanced sensor fusion and robust control algorithms enable the drone to efficiently handle complex environmental scenarios, greatly enhancing mission success.

Based on the PX4 platform—originally developed from the PIXHAWK project at ETH Zurich—PIX2.4.8 is acclaimed as a leading open-source flight controller, providing cost-effective yet high-performance autopilot solutions for academia, hobbyists, and industry. Managed by the Dronecode Foundation under the BSD license, it supports commercial applications in visual navigation, multi-drone coordination, and target tracking.

Compared to our previously considered F4V3SPLUS, PIX2.4.8 offers superior computational power, enhanced sensor integration, and greater flight stability. Its mature firmware and comprehensive mission-planning tools simplify development, significantly improving project reliability and efficiency.

光流optical flow sensor（185词）：

The major reason why we picked PX4FLOW v1.3.1 optical flow sensor was that it has an ability to deliver precise ground speed measurements and stable positioning even in low-GPS environment, which is quite suitable for the in-door test site. This ability is achieved by using high-resolution imaging paired with a built-in 3-axis gyroscope to calculate motion along the X and Y axes, ensuring accurate hovering and navigation.  

To be more specific, the PX4FLOW sensor contributes to these key tasks in our project:

Task 1 (Take-off): It enables a smooth vertical take-off and rapid stabilization into a hover.

Task 2 (Tunnelling): It ensures the drone maintains a precise trajectory through narrow spaces.

Task 3 (Obstacle Avoidance): It supports accurate horizontal positioning, allowing the drone to safely navigate around obstacles.

Tasks 4 & 5 (Package Search, Pickup, and Drop-off): It provides the precision needed for accurate package handling.

Task 6 (Landing and Communication): It assists in controlled descent and stable landing, ensuring reliable communication post-landing.

Overall, its ability to operate without GPS, combined with its high-precision and robust performance, makes the PX4FLOW sensor indispensable for our project.

遥控器remote control（142词）

We selected the Radiolink AT9S Pro primarily for its reliability and comprehensive control features during our initial flight tests. With 12 full-function channels, it provides enough flexibility to evaluate maneuvers necessary for various mission tasks—such as Task 1 (Take-off), Task 3 (Obstacle Avoidance), or Tasks 4 & 5 (Package Search, Pickup, and Drop-off)—before moving on to autonomous, program-based control. Its advanced FHSS and DSSS16 frequency-hopping protocols provide robust anti-interference capabilities, ensuring stable communication and more accurate test results.

Beyond signal stability, the AT9S Pro also integrates a 32-bit ARM MCU and TI’s CC2533 wireless module for heightened responsiveness and range, which is especially helpful in early-stage trials when adjustments and troubleshooting are common. Once these tests confirm the drone’s ability to perform each required task, the remote is no longer needed, and the flight controller transitions to fully automated operation.

接收器receiver(160词)

We chose the R9DS receiver for its compact size (just 10.5g) and the option of 9 or 10 channels, depending on whether it’s set to PWM or SBUS mode. This setup pairs well with the Radiolink AT9S Pro transmitter we selected and delivers the precision we need during initial tests for Task 1 (Take-off), Task 3 (Obstacle Avoidance) and Tasks 4 & 5 (Package Search, Pickup, and Drop-off).

Moreover, a standout feature of the R9DS is its dependable range—reaching up to 4000 meters under optimal conditions—thanks to FHSS frequency hopping on the 2.4GHz ISM band. This method ensures a stable signal and reduces interference, improving control consistency across different testing environments. The receiver can also relay telemetry data, such as battery voltage, when paired with a PRM module, and it accommodates a wide input voltage range (3.6–12V). These attributes provide stable flight performance, easier troubleshooting, and a smoother transition from manual test flights to fully autonomous missions.
